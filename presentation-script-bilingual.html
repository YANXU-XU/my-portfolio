0 · 上台前 60 秒检查清单 60 s Pre-flight checklist

  • 打开 index.html，语言确认在中文。 Open index.html; make sure the language is Chinese (top-right switch).

1 · 开场 约 40 秒 Opening

Action停在首页，先不说话，让自动巡演跑两个动作。 Stay on the homepage. Say nothing for a moment; let the auto-tour play two motions.

"大家好。开始之前我想先问一个问题：如果要你用语言描述'走路'，你会怎么说？

"Hi everyone. Before I start, one question: if you had to describe 'walking' in words, what would you say?

（停顿两秒，等观众反应 · pause two seconds, let the room answer）

大概就是'两条腿交替往前'，一句话就说完了。可如果真要让一个火柴人走起来，需要的是一连串非常具体的数字：髋在哪儿、大腿转多少度、小腿什么时候弯、脚掌什么时候落地。

Probably 'the legs move forward alternately' — one sentence and you're done. But to actually make a stickman walk, you need a long list of very specific numbers: where the hip sits, how many degrees the thigh turns, when the knee bends, when the foot lands.

所以我会说：动作是这个世界上最难说清楚、也最容易被看错的东西。

So I'd say: motion is one of the hardest things in the world to put into words — and one of the easiest to misread.

我做的这个网站，就是想把它变得可以说清楚——一个能把动作拆开看、拆开改、再存下来的地方。

This site is my attempt to make it speakable: a place where a movement can be taken apart, changed, and kept.

Action鼠标沿顶部导航从左到右划过去，不要点。 Sweep the mouse along the top navigation, left to right. Don't click.

"它叫 Stickman Motion Simulator，火柴人动作模拟器。一共五页：首页、动作游戏、动作画廊、动画编辑器、关于。

"It's called Stickman Motion Simulator. Five pages: Home, Simulator, Gallery, Animator, About.

结构不是五个并列的页面，而是三层递进：

These aren't five parallel pages — they're three steps:

  • 看——首页和画廊，先认识七个动作。 See — Home and Gallery: meet the seven motions.
  • 玩——动作游戏，逼你把这七个动作记住。 Play — the Simulator: it forces you to remember them.
  • 做——动画编辑器，让你自己造一个动作出来。 Make — the Animator: it lets you build one yourself.

今天重点在最后两层，尤其是'做'这一层。

Today I'll focus on the last two — especially 'Make'.

3 · 第一层：七个动作，就是一套词汇 约 50 秒 Layer one — seven motions are a vocabulary

Action进「动作画廊」，点「走路」卡片的 播放动作，再点「挥手」。 Open the Gallery; press Play motion on the walk card, then on the wave card.

"先看画廊。七个动作：站、走、跑、跳、挥手、蹲、游泳。

"Start with the Gallery. Seven motions: stand, walk, run, jump, wave, squat, swim.

它们全部只用圆、矩形、圆角粗线拼出来，颜色也只有固定的八种。每个动作有自己的节奏和声音——（点播放）点'播放动作'就能看到。

They're built from nothing but circles, rectangles and rounded thick lines, with a fixed palette of eight colors. Each one has its own rhythm and sound — (play) press 'Play motion' and you'll see.

我特意让它们的画法完全一致。因为接下来你会发现：这七个动作不是插图，是这个网站的词汇表。记住了它们，游戏才玩得下去，编辑器才有东西可改。

I deliberately drew them the same way. Because you'll notice: these seven aren't illustrations — they're the site's vocabulary. Know them, and the game works; and the editor has something to change.

4 · 第二层：动作游戏，让用户"记住" 约 70 秒 Layer two — the game that makes you remember

Action进「动作游戏」，点 Start game，双手离开键盘，让观众先看它演。 Open the Simulator, press Start game, keep your hands off the keys and let the room watch.

"第二层，动作游戏，里面这个模式叫 Echo——回声。

"Layer two: the Simulator, and inside it a mode called Echo.

规则很简单：它先演一段动作序列，然后你要用数字键 1 到 7，按同样的顺序复现一遍。第一轮 3 个动作，之后每过一轮多一个，最长 7 个，一共五轮。三条命，错三次结束。

The rule is simple: it performs a sequence, and you reproduce it with number keys 1 to 7. Round one has three motions, then one more each round, up to seven — five rounds in all. Three lives; three misses and it's over.

Action演完后按对前两个，第三个故意按错。 When playback ends, enter the first two correctly — then miss the third on purpose.

（边按边说）1 是站，2 是走，3 是跑……

(pressing as you speak) 1 is stand, 2 is walk, 3 is run…

（故意按错，停半秒让观众看到反馈 · miss on purpose, hold half a second so the room sees the feedback）

我故意错一个。注意它没有只扣分——它会把正确答案重演一遍，同时把每一格进度标出来。

I missed one on purpose. Notice it doesn't just take a life — it replays the correct answer and marks the progress of every step.

Action指一下进度条和分数。 Point at the progress strip and the score.

这里是我做这个游戏真正想达到的效果：玩家在不知不觉中，把七个动作和七个编号绑在一起了。

This is what I actually wanted from the game: players bind seven motions to seven numbers without noticing.

所以他才不会在下一步、面对一台'机器'的时候，完全不知道从哪开始。

So that in the next step, standing in front of a machine, they aren't completely lost.

这是第二层：先让人认识动作。接下来是今天的正题。

That's layer two: learn to recognize motion. Now the main event.

5 · 第三层：动画编辑器，从"复现"到"创作" 约 3 分 30 秒 · 全程重点 Layer three — the Animator: from reproducing to creating

Action进「动画编辑器」。停两秒，让观众看清骨架。 Open the Animator. Pause for two seconds and let the room look at the skeleton.

"同样七个动作，换一个方向：从'复现它'变成'造一个'。

"Same seven motions, opposite direction: from 'reproduce it' to 'make one'.

左边这副火柴人，不是一张图片，是一副真的骨架。我接下来会拆四件事给大家看：它怎么连、脚怎么落地、动起来靠什么、以及怎么变成作品。

This stickman on the left isn't a picture — it's a real skeleton. I'll take apart four things: how it's connected, how the foot lands, what makes it move, and how it becomes a piece of work.

5.1 · 它是关节，不是图片 约 35 秒 — It's joints, not a picture

Action先点右上臂，直接拖动；再选躯干，拖一下，让头、肩、手臂一起动。 Click the right upper arm and drag; then select the torso and rotate it so head, shoulders and arms follow.

"一共十段可以选：头、躯干、两条上臂、两条前臂、两条大腿、两条小腿。每一段都挂在一个关节上，所以动一段，它的'孩子'会跟着走。

"Ten selectable segments: head, torso, two upper arms, two forearms, two thighs, two shins. Each hangs on a joint, so when one moves, its children follow.

（拖躯干）我转躯干，头、肩膀、两条手臂全都跟上了——这就是层级，和动画软件里的父子关系是一回事，只不过这里是手写出来的。

(rotating the torso) I turn the torso, and the head, both shoulders and both arms come with it — that's hierarchy, the same parent-child idea as animation software, except this one is hand-written.

另外每一段都有自己的活动范围：躯干只允许 -25 到 40 度，前臂不能反折，小腿不能往前踢。因为真实的关节就长这样，全部放开反而假。

Each segment also has its own range: the torso only allows -25 to 40 degrees, the forearm can't hyperextend, the shin can't kick forward. Real joints work that way; removing the limits actually looks worse.

5.2 · 脚会自己落地 约 40 秒 — The foot lands by itself

Action选一条大腿，把它抬起来，让观众盯着脚和地面阴影。 Select a thigh and lift it; get the room to watch the foot and its ground shadow.

"第二件事，请看脚下。（抬腿）我把腿抬起来——脚掌还是水平的，地上的影子变淡、变窄了。

"Second, look at the foot. (lifting the leg) I lift the leg — the foot stays level, and its shadow on the ground fades and narrows.

这个脚不是我一张张画出来的，是算出来的。我知道髋在哪、大腿 80、小腿 74，剩下就是一个三角形求解——我做的是'两骨 IK'：目标点在地面，膝盖朝前弯。

That foot isn't drawn frame by frame — it's solved. I know where the hip is, the thigh is 80, the shin is 74 — the rest is a triangle. It's a two-bone IK solve: the target is the ground, the knee bends forward.

为什么非做不可？因为如果只按角度转，脚会浮在空中、或者插进地里，一眼就假。让脚自己去找地面，整套动作立刻就有了重量。

Why bother? Because if you only rotate by angle, the foot floats in the air or sinks into the ground, and it reads as fake instantly. Let the foot find the ground, and the whole motion suddenly has weight.

5.3 · 预设是时间轴，不是动图 约 35 秒 — Presets are timelines, not GIFs

Action点预设 Run，播放中把速度切到 Fast，再点 Jump。 Pick the Run preset, switch speed to Fast while it plays, then pick Jump.

"如果每个姿势都靠手拖，太慢了。所以给了六个预设：站、走、跑、跳、游泳、蹲。

"Doing every pose by hand would be too slow, so there are six presets: stand, walk, run, jump, swim, squat.

关键在于：这些预设不是循环播放的动图，是关键帧。跑是 0.7 秒一个循环，走是 1 秒，跳是 1.8 秒而且只播一次。

The key point: these aren't looping GIFs — they're keyframes. A run cycle is 0.7 s, a walk is 1 s, and a jump is 1.8 s and plays once.

因为每一帧都是现场算出来的，所以速度可以随便改——（切到 Fast）从慢到快，动作是重新计算的，不是把视频快进。

Because every frame is computed live, speed can change freely — (switch to Fast) slow to fast, recomputed, not fast-forwarded.

5.4 · 撤销栈是一条线 约 30 秒 — Undo is one clean line

Action故意连续拖两下，Ctrl+Z 两次，再 Ctrl+Y。 Drag twice on purpose, press Ctrl+Z twice, then Ctrl+Y.

"手摆姿势一定会手滑。Ctrl+Z 撤销，Ctrl+Y 重做，历史上限 60 步。

"Hand-posing always ends in a slip. Ctrl+Z to undo, Ctrl+Y to redo, sixty steps of history.

这里有个真实的坑，我待会儿会讲：拖动一次会产生几百个中间状态，如果每个都进历史，撤销就变成'一格一格往回退'。所以我把 600 毫秒内的连续拖动合并成一步。

There's a real trap here, which I'll come back to: one drag produces hundreds of intermediate states. If each one entered the history, undo would step back pixel by pixel. So continuous dragging within 600 ms is merged into one step.

5.5 · 从摆姿势到有作品 约 60 秒 · 全篇高潮 — From posing to a finished take

Action按顺序：● Record → 拖两个关节 → Pause rec. → 再动一下 → Resume rec. → 再拖一个 → Stop rec. → Play recording → 输入名字 → Save recording → 刷新页面。 Sequence: Record → drag two joints → Pause → drag again → Resume → drag once more → Stop → Play recording → type a name → Save recording → refresh the page.

"最后是我最想给大家看的一步：录下来。

"And here's the step I most want to show you: recording it.

（点 Record）现在开始录制。我随便摆两个姿势……（拖两下）

(Record) Recording now. I'll make a couple of poses… (drag twice)

好，我先暂停。注意——暂停的时候我怎么改，都不会进录像，这是故意的，方便你停下来想一想。（拖一下，再 Resume）

Now I'll pause. Note that anything I change while paused does not go into the take — that's deliberate, so you can stop and think. (drag once, then Resume)

继续。（再拖一下）停。

Continue. (drag once more) Stop.

它现在告诉我：可以播放，也可以保存。（点播放）它按刚才的时间顺序重演了一遍，连我中间停顿的那一段都在里面。

It now offers two things: play it back, or save it. (play) It replays in the order I made it — including the pause in the middle.

（输入名字 Wave hello，保存；然后刷新页面，指向列表）刷新之后，它还在。因为这条录像存在浏览器的本地存储里。没有服务器，也不用登录；换一台电脑就没有了——这是我刻意的取舍，这个网站不收集任何人的数据。

(Type Wave hello, save; then refresh and point at the list) After a refresh it's still there — because the take lives in the browser's local storage. No server, no login; change computer and it's gone. That's a deliberate trade-off: this site collects nobody's data.

旁边那个'姿势库'是同一套机制：单个姿势最多存 50 条，录像最多 40 条，每条最长 60 秒。

The pose library beside it works the same way: up to 50 single poses, 40 takes, each up to 60 seconds.

Optional时间富余时：点「导出」，说明下载的是 JSON。 If time allows: press Export and explain that it downloads JSON.

另外它还能把当前姿势导出成 JSON，里面就是每一段的角度。不是我自己玩的格式，是可以直接喂给别的动画程序的。

It can also export the current pose as JSON — an angle per segment. Not a format I invented for myself; you can feed it to other animation tools.

5.6 · 容易被忽略的设计：回放时编辑器是锁的 约 20 秒 — Playback locks the editor

Action播放一条录像，指着一片灰掉的按钮。 Play a take and point at the row of greyed-out controls.

"看，播放的时候，预设、滑杆、撤销、保存，全都灰了。

"Watch this: while a take plays, the presets, sliders, undo and save all go grey.

这不是没做完，是故意锁的。因为回放本身在不停地写姿态，如果这时候用户还能拖，两套逻辑就会抢同一个状态，画面会抖得像坏了。所以回放期间，编辑器只读。

That isn't unfinished — it's deliberate. Playback is writing poses every frame; if the user could drag at the same time, two systems would fight over one state and the picture would jitter like it's broken. So during playback, the editor is read-only.

6 · 制作路程：四步，每一步都被上一步的问题逼出来 约 1 分 20 秒 How it was made — four steps, each forced by the last

Action停在编辑器，或退回首页，边讲边随手拖一下骨架（手里有动作，说话更自然）。 Stay in the Animator (or go back to Home) and keep a hand moving on the skeleton while you talk.

"最后说说它是怎么做出来的。整个过程大概四步，不是我一开始就想好的，每一步都是被上一步的问题逼出来的。

"Finally, how it was built. About four steps — and I didn't plan them; each one was forced by a problem in the previous one.

第一步，我本来只想画七个动作。最早是纯 SVG，手写坐标，让七个小人动起来。做完就发现：我只会'播放'，不会'修改'。这就是第一个念头——如果用户想改呢？

Step one: I only wanted to draw seven motions. It began as pure SVG, with hand-written coordinates, seven little figures moving. Then I noticed: I could play, but I couldn't change anything. That was the first thought — what if the user wants to change it?

第二步，把它改成骨架。所有部位从'写死的坐标'变成'关节 + 角度'的数据。这一步是整个项目的分水岭：动作从图形变成了数据，而数据可以被计算、被撤销、被保存。后面所有功能，都是靠这一步才成立的。

Step two: turn it into a skeleton. Every part went from hard-coded coordinates to joint + angle data. That's the watershed of this project: motion stopped being a picture and became data — and data can be computed, undone and stored. Everything after it depends on this one change.

第三步，加游戏和编辑器。游戏解决'不知道怎么开始'，编辑器解决'只能看不能改'。它们看起来是两个东西，其实共用同一份动作数据。

Step three: add the game and the editor. The game solves 'I don't know how to start'; the editor solves 'I can only watch'. They look like two products, but they share the same motion data.

第四步，磨到能给别人用。这一步花的时间比前三步加起来还多：中英文两套文案三百多条，我写脚本检查有没有漏翻、有没有空字符串；键盘能选中部位、状态变化会播报给读屏软件；系统开了'减少动态效果'就关掉动画；本地数据读不出来的时候先改名备份、再重置，绝不当场覆盖。

Step four: polish it until someone else can use it. This took longer than the first three steps combined: 300+ strings in Chinese and English, with a script that checks for missing or empty translations; the keyboard can select segments, and state changes are announced to screen readers; reduced-motion settings stop the animation; and when saved data can't be read, it is renamed and backed up before a reset — never overwritten in place.

Pick 1–2三个真实的坑，按剩余时间挑一到两个讲。 Three real pitfalls — tell one or two depending on time.

"讲三个我印象最深的坑：

"Three problems I remember most:

一，脚会浮空。最早的版本脚是跟着小腿转的，人一动就'飘'。这就是刚才那个 IK 的由来。

One: the foot floated. In the first version the foot simply rotated with the shin, so the moment the figure moved, it drifted. That's where the IK came from.

二，撤销会卡死。连续拖动时每个像素都入栈，60 步一秒就满了，所以做了时间合并。合并之后还冒出个新 bug：如果在合并窗口内按撤销，那个延迟提交会把重做记录冲掉——这个我后来单独修了。

Two: undo froze. While dragging, every pixel entered the stack and sixty steps filled up in a second, so I merged by time. That produced a new bug: pressing undo inside the merge window let a delayed commit wipe the redo stack — I fixed that separately.

三，回放和编辑会打架。一开始允许边放边改，结果两套逻辑交替覆盖姿态，画面像坏了。最后决定：回放期间一律只读。

Three: playback and editing fought. At first you could edit while it played, and the two systems overwrote each other's poses; it looked broken. The final rule: playback is strictly read-only.

7 · 收束 约 30 秒 Closing

Action回到首页，让自动巡演继续跑。 Return to the homepage and let the auto-tour keep running.

"总结一下。这个网站做的事其实很小：把'动作'从一个说不清的词，变成一串可以看、可以改、可以存下来的数字。

"To sum up. What this site does is small: it turns 'motion' from a word you can't quite say into a string of numbers you can see, change, and keep.

三层结构对应三句话：画廊让你看见动作，游戏让你记住动作，编辑器让你造出动作。

Three layers, three sentences: the gallery lets you see motion, the game lets you remember it, the editor lets you make it.

技术上它很朴素——没有框架，没有构建工具，就是 HTML、CSS、SVG 和原生 JavaScript，双击文件就能跑；五个页面、一个样式表、六个脚本，没有一行第三方依赖。

Technically it's plain: no framework, no build step — HTML, CSS, SVG and vanilla JavaScript; it runs by double-clicking a file. Five pages, one stylesheet, six scripts, zero third-party dependencies.

但我觉得它至少证明了一件事：最简陋的素材——几个圆、几根线——只要你愿意把它拆到关节这一层，它就能承载很复杂的东西。

But I think it proves one thing: the humblest material — a few circles, a few lines — can carry something complex, if you're willing to take it down to the joint.

谢谢大家。

Thank you.

10 · 可能的提问与备答 Q&A prep Likely questions and short answers

问题 / Question回答要点 / Answer
为什么不用 React / Three.js？
Why not React or Three.js?		 目标是"打开就能读、就能跑"：无依赖、无构建步骤；二维骨骼用 SVG 的 transform 足够表达。 The goal is a project you can open and read and run: no dependencies, no build step. SVG transform is enough for a 2D skeleton.
数据存在哪里？隐私呢？
Where is the data? What about privacy?		 浏览器 localStorage，两个键（姿势 / 录像），明文、留在本机、不上传。 Browser localStorage, two keys (poses / takes), plain text, local only, never uploaded.
为什么只有侧视？
Why side view only?		 侧视是动画最基础的视图，两骨 IK 在侧视下有解析解；换成正视图或三维，复杂度会立刻上一个量级。 The side view is the most basic animation view, and two-bone IK has a closed-form solution in it. A front view or 3D would raise the complexity by an order of magnitude.
最难的部分是什么？
What was the hardest part?		 不是画图，是状态：录制、暂停、回放、编辑四种模式必须互斥，锁的顺序错一步就会互相覆盖。 Not the drawing — the state: record, pause, replay and edit must be mutually exclusive; get the locking order wrong and they overwrite each other.
接下来会加什么？
What comes next?		 时间轴与曲线编辑、把录像接进画廊变成"我的作品"、导出更多格式。 A timeline with curve editing, feeding takes back into the gallery as "my work", and more export formats.
为什么是七个动作？
Why seven motions?		 "最小可玩的词汇量"：够组成 3 到 7 个动作的句子，又不至于记不住。 The smallest playable vocabulary: enough to form sentences of three to seven, few enough to remember.
无障碍做了什么？
What about accessibility?		 键盘可选中与撤销、状态用 aria-live 播报、跳过导航、支持"减少动态效果"、中英切换。 Keyboard selection and undo, state announced via aria-live, skip links, reduced-motion support, and a Chinese/English switch.
