About the motion study

Seven ways to
move and play.

Stickman Motion Simulator turns simple body positions into a bright, playful movement study. Each pose gives a tiny character a different rhythm, direction, and attitude.

✦

Movement vocabulary

Stand, walk, run, jump, wave, squat, and swim are the simulator's seven basic actions. Together they show how small changes in balance and limb direction can make motion feel calm, quick, friendly, or funny.

⌁

Motion in a loop

Click the stickman to discover a new action, then watch the automatic tour continue through the sequence. The fixed voice note changes with every pose, giving each movement its own playful personality.

↗

Keep moving

There is no perfect pose in this little motion lab. Try the simulator, compare the action gallery, and notice how a few colorful shapes can suggest a whole body in motion.
